HR Interview Preparation

Practice common HR interview questions and improve your confidence before placement interviews.

HR Question

Tell me about yourself.

Take a moment to prepare your answer. Try to speak clearly and confidently.

Interview Tip

Keep Your Introduction Structured

Start with your name and education, mention your important skills or projects, and finish with your career goal.

Common Questions
  • Tell me about yourself.
  • What are your strengths?
  • What are your weaknesses?
  • Why should we hire you?
  • Where do you see yourself in five years?
